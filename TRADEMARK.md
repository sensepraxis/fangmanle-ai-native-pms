## 3. Forks and derivatives

You may fork, modify, and redistribute **code** under the applicable software licenses, but you must **not**:

1. Use 房满乐 / Fangmanle as a product name, company name, domain, store listing, confusing repository name, logo, or primary marketing brand.
2. Imply the fork is official Fangmanle, or is produced, endorsed, or affiliated with SensePraxis.
3. Keep UI/docs/marketing that would make the public think the software is still the Fangmanle product (including an unmodified product title on the landing page).
4. After a fork, use **your own brand**. A factual, non-confusing credit is OK, e.g. “based on an open-source hotel PMS framework” — not “official Fangmanle” or “Fangmanle fork edition”.

Code can be forked. The **Fangmanle trademark cannot**.
